## Channel permission before sending

Reddit prohibits repeated or unsolicited mass engagement and specifically calls
out repetitive promotion and tools that proliferate spam. Community moderators
also decide what unwanted promotion means locally. Check each community's current
rules and the exact proposed placement; permission must cover commercial links
and the actual operator/agent identity. [Reddit spam policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam)

Current per-community permission is **unverified**. An authorized operator first
records a rule permalink or an explicit moderator permission receipt for the
exact commercial invitation. Any moderator contact itself requires the owner's
explicit communication authorization. No permission means no message, no private
DM, no profile stalking, and no workaround account. Prefer an approved opt-in
offer/help thread with voluntary responses. An offer-thread post alone does not
prove that these five people saw it. Address a candidate directly only if both
the placement rules and their invitation/consent permit it.

If these specific candidates cannot be reached permissibly, mark them
`NOT_CONTACTABLE` and replace each with a named, current opt-in prospect and a
public evidence link. Do not silently count community membership, impressions,
upvotes, or five copies of the same post as qualified exposure. If five lawful
exposures cannot be obtained under the bounded plan, report the distribution
gate unmet; do not claim that the checkout validates demand.

## One bounded, operator-executable campaign

This is a proposed seven-day experiment after explicit final authorization.
No continuing founder posting, sales calls, bespoke consulting, or paid media
is assumed. The owner makes a one-time decision on channel/offer/payment scope;
an authorized BeanLabs operator performs the finite steps and routine fulfillment.

1. **Preflight:** verify activation receipts, supported-input prevalidation,
   catalog freshness, safe delivery/retention, corrections/refunds, real fee
   schedule, and private fulfillment readiness. Publish only the owner-approved
   sample/contract/intake destination; never expose private report storage.
   Record `campaign_id=BF-CER-5-v1` and freeze USD 12 and the sample revision.
2. **Permission and freshness:** spend at most 20 operator minutes checking the
   five candidates and exact channel rules. Save a minimal dated source/rule
   receipt per candidate. Do not retain whole profiles or unrelated comments.
   If permission needs founder relationship-building, this channel fails the
   low-founder-labor requirement.
3. **Five exposures:** after communication authorization and permission checks,
   issue at most one tailored invitation per eligible candidate, in priority
   order. Tailor only the documented device/workload and scope caveat. No
   reminder to nonresponders. No public tagging campaign. An operator may reply
   to an opt-in response once with the approved sample and checkout path.
4. **Self-service order:** sample → scope acknowledgement → device/use-case
   validation → USD 12 checkout → verified payment event → durable order claim
   → private report delivery. Nonempty constraints, unsupported hardware,
   unsupported context, or required services outside scope go to decline/review
   before checkout. Do not silently narrow the frozen contract to bypass its
   unresolved fulfillment gate.
5. **Measure once:** at day seven, report candidate outcomes, exposure evidence,
   explicit intent, orders, delivery, refunds, settled/available funds, fully
   loaded costs, and time. No ongoing campaign or schedule is created by this
   plan. Follow-up recurrence, if later authorized, must use the approved
   scheduler and remain a separate implementation decision.

For persistence without founder effort, reuse the approved sample and a static
searchable offer page after publication authority. A future Apify listing may
add marketplace/API discovery after its separate pricing, payout, and privacy
gates. Neither passive source guarantees traffic. Do not substitute indefinite
content production for evidence that five qualified people considered the offer.

Keep acquisition data separate from payment/fulfillment records. Record only
`campaign_id`, prospect ID, public source, checked time, permission receipt,
approved message hash, exposure receipt, response category, offer version,
and a privacy-safe order reference. Public handles do not belong in the cash

| Promotion eligible | Unrelated payer, reconciled first WITHDRAWABLE/WITHDRAWN event, fees/costs/refunds netted once |

Freeze immediately on uncertain permission, rejected posting, customer-secret
input, reconciliation mismatch, false delivery promise, privacy failure, or
refund/dispute outside the tested control. No retry through a different channel.
Pause after two negative fully loaded orders, as the contract requires. Escalate
an exception over ten minutes; do not turn it into ongoing founder fulfillment.

At five verified exposures or seven days, whichever occurs first: zero intent
and zero orders means **REPOSITION**, with no feature build or price change
until a new bounded test is reviewed. Fewer than five verified exposures means
**DISTRIBUTION INCOMPLETE**, not demand rejection. One purchase alone is not
PILOT: the runbook additionally requires two other explicit purchase-intent
confirmations, nonnegative fully loaded contribution, and the unrelated payment
reconciled withdrawable/withdrawn. The finite five-person test cannot establish
repeatability or a reliable acquisition rate.
